## 1. PURPOSE

This standard defines how affiliate links must be identified and implemented within Hidden History.

It applies to current vehicle-parts sourcing and to any future affiliate functionality.

---

## 2. CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION

Hidden History may display vehicle-parts links alongside relevant MOT failures or advisories.

The current third-party service associated with parts sourcing is eBay.

Only an actually active and approved affiliate arrangement may be presented as an affiliate relationship.

---

## 3. DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENT

Where a link may generate commission for Hidden History, the user must be given a clear and understandable disclosure.

The disclosure should appear at or sufficiently close to the relevant link or product presentation.

Preferred wording:

> **Affiliate link — Hidden History may earn a commission if you purchase through this link.**

A shorter equivalent may be used where the context remains clear.

---

## 4. PLACEMENT

The disclosure must not be hidden solely in:

- The general Privacy Policy
- A distant help page
- An unrelated terms document
- A settings page
- A footer that a user is unlikely to connect with the relevant link

The user should be able to understand the commercial nature of the link before or at the point they interact with it.

---

## 5. NO MISREPRESENTATION

Affiliate links must not be described as:

- Official government links
- DVLA or DVSA links unless they actually are
- Independent recommendations where the commercial relationship makes that misleading
- Proof that a part is required
- Confirmation of a mechanical diagnosis
- Confirmation that a vehicle is unsafe
- Guaranteed compatible products

---

## 6. PART COMPATIBILITY

A link generated from an MOT advisory or failure is an aid to finding a potential part.

It is not a guarantee that the linked product is the correct part.

Where practical, the interface should encourage the user to verify compatibility before purchase.

---

## 7. COMMERCIAL NEUTRALITY

Affiliate relationships must not alter:

- Source vehicle data
- MOT information
- Analysis rules
- Risk results
- Advert-analysis findings
- Safety conclusions

Commercial links are an optional downstream service and must remain separate from the underlying analysis.

---

## 8. PRODUCT PRESENTATION

If a product or supplier is displayed, the presentation must not create a misleading impression about:

- Price
- Availability
- Compatibility
- Quality
- Independence
- Endorsement

Any commercial ranking or ordering must have a genuine service rationale and must not be described as an independent recommendation if that would be misleading.

---

## 9. TRACKING

Affiliate tracking must be kept separate from Hidden History's core vehicle analysis.

Hidden History currently operates without general-purpose analytics or advertising tracking.

Before adding affiliate technology that stores or accesses information on the user's device or uses identifiers for non-essential tracking, the privacy and PECR position must be assessed and any required consent implemented.

---

## 10. PRIVACY DISCLOSURE

Where affiliate technology involves personal information, the Privacy Policy, Cookie and Similar Technologies Policy and Data Protection Register must be updated before release.

The actual categories of data, recipients and purposes must be documented.

---

## 11. PARTNER APPROVAL

A new affiliate partner must not be treated as active until:

- Commercial approval exists
- The integration is technically live
- The disclosure has been implemented
- The partner has been entered in AFFILIATE_PARTNER_REGISTER.md
- Relevant terms and privacy arrangements have been reviewed

---

## 12. TESTING

Before release of an affiliate integration, test:

- Disclosure visibility
- Link destination
- Correct affiliate attribution
- Correct non-affiliate fallback behaviour
- No accidental leakage of unrelated user information
- No misleading compatibility claim
- Correct behaviour for free and Pro users
- Removal/disablement if the partner arrangement ends
