## 1. PURPOSE

This document defines how affiliate links must be identified and
implemented within Hidden History.

---

## 2. CURRENT AFFILIATE FUNCTIONALITY

Hidden History may display affiliate links alongside vehicle-part
information.

For example, a user may view an MOT failure or advisory and receive a
link to a relevant vehicle-parts supplier.

Affiliate links may be available to:

- Free users
- Paid/Pro users

## 8. NO MISREPRESENTATION

Affiliate links must not be presented as:

- Official government links
- Independent recommendations where that would be misleading
- Evidence that a particular part is definitely required
- Confirmation of mechanical diagnosis

---

## 9. PART COMPATIBILITY

A linked product must not be represented as guaranteed compatible unless
compatibility has actually been established.

Users should verify compatibility before purchase.
