# HIDDEN HISTORY
# VEHICLE DATA CORRECTION AND SOURCE DISPUTE POLICY

Status: PRE-BETA / WORKING DRAFT

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This policy explains the distinction between:

1. Information generated or processed by Hidden History; and
2. Information originating from an external vehicle-data source.

---

## 2. SOURCE DATA

Hidden History may retrieve information from:

- DVLA
- DVSA/MOT
- Other authorised data sources introduced in future

Hidden History may normalise or display information returned by those
sources.

---

## 3. SOURCE RECORD CORRECTIONS

Where the underlying source is responsible for the record, Hidden
History may not be able to directly alter the original source record.

A user may therefore need to contact the relevant source.

---

## 4. HIDDEN HISTORY PROCESSING ERRORS

If Hidden History incorrectly:

- Displays information
- Maps information
- Formats information
- Associates information with the wrong report
- Produces an incorrect analysis

the issue should be investigated by Hidden History.

---

## 5. REPORTING AN ERROR

Users should provide:

- Account email where applicable
- Vehicle registration
- Description of the suspected error
- Screenshot where appropriate
- Relevant supporting evidence

## 6. INVESTIGATION

Hidden History should determine:

1. What information was received from the source.
2. What information was stored.
3. What information was displayed.
4. Whether the error originated from the source or Hidden History.
5. Whether correction is possible.

---

## 7. SOURCE-DATA DISPUTES

Where the underlying government or external record is disputed, Hidden
History may direct the user to the relevant source authority.

Hidden History should not falsely represent itself as being able to
alter an external government's records.

---

## 8. ANALYSIS CORRECTIONS

If an analysis result is incorrect because Hidden History incorrectly
processed available information, the issue should be reviewed and
corrected where appropriate.

---

## 9. HISTORICAL REPORTS

Where a saved report reflects information that was accurate when
retrieved but the underlying source later changes, Hidden History should
determine whether:

- The saved report remains a historical snapshot; or
- The report is refreshed.
