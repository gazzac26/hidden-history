This register records the contractual position for organisations that process personal information on behalf of Hidden History.

## 3. PROCESSOR CONTRACT CHECKS

Where an organisation is a processor for Hidden History, the applicable arrangement must address the Article 28 requirements, including as applicable:

- Subject matter and duration
- Nature and purpose of processing
- Types of personal data
- Categories of data subjects
- Controller rights and obligations
- Documented processing instructions
- Confidentiality
- Security measures
- Subprocessor controls
- Assistance with data-subject rights
- Assistance with security and breach obligations
- DPIA/consultation assistance where relevant
- Deletion or return of personal data at end of processing
- Audit/inspection and compliance information

---

## 4. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Where personal information is transferred outside the UK, the actual production arrangement must be assessed and documented.

Record where applicable:

- Destination country
- Provider entity
- Transfer mechanism
- Adequacy position
- Contractual safeguards
- Any transfer risk assessment required
- Relevant supplementary measures

No international-transfer safeguard is assumed merely because a provider is widely used.

---

## 5. PROVIDER DUE DILIGENCE

Before relying on a processor, Hidden History should assess whether the provider offers sufficient guarantees for appropriate technical and organisational measures.

The assessment should consider the nature and risk of the processing and the provider's security, reliability and contractual position.

---

## 6. MISSING OR UNCERTAIN ARRANGEMENTS

If a required processor agreement or contractual safeguard cannot be verified:

1. Identify the provider;
2. Identify the processing involved;
3. Obtain the current provider terms;
4. Determine whether a processor arrangement exists;
5. Confirm applicable contractual safeguards;
6. Record the result;
7. Escalate before beta if the processing cannot lawfully or safely proceed.
