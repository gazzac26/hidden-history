3. ACCOUNT DATA

Data Category

Hidden History may process:

- email address;
- account identifier;
- authentication-related information;
- user-provided profile information necessary for account functionality; and
- account status information.

Hidden History does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information for profiling or commercial purposes.

Purposes

Account information is processed for:

- account creation;
- authentication;
- account management;
- providing account-based functionality;
- protecting accounts;
- service security;
- preventing abuse; and
- responding to support or account-related requests.

Lawful Basis

The applicable lawful basis may include:

Contract — where processing is necessary to provide the requested account or service functionality.

Legitimate interests — where processing is necessary for security, abuse prevention, service protection or other legitimate operational purposes and those interests are not overridden by the individual's rights and interests.

Legal obligation — where processing is necessary to comply with a legal requirement.

The selected lawful basis must correspond to the actual purpose of each processing activity. The ICO states that contract processing must be necessary for performing the contract or taking a requested pre-contractual step; processing that is merely useful to the business should not automatically be treated as contract processing.

Retention

Account information is retained while the account remains active or for as long as necessary to provide the associated service.

When an account is deleted, account-linked information will be deleted unless Hidden History has a lawful reason to retain particular information for a limited period.

4. VEHICLE SEARCH DATA

Data Category

Vehicle-related information processed by Hidden History may include:

- vehicle registration number;
- vehicle information obtained from applicable vehicle-data sources;
- MOT information;
- MOT history;
- MOT failures;
- MOT advisories;
- vehicle analysis results;
- advert information where submitted for analysis;
- report-generation timestamp; and
- saved report information where the user chooses to save a report.

Important Search Retention Rule

A normal unsaved vehicle search is not retained as a user search history record by Hidden History.

The application retrieves the requested information, processes it and displays the result.

If the user chooses to save a report, the relevant report information is stored in the user's account.

Purposes

Vehicle information is processed for:

- providing vehicle searches;
- displaying vehicle information;
- analysing vehicle information;
- providing vehicle safety and purchasing information;
- generating vehicle reports;
- providing MOT information;
- providing advert analysis;
- supporting Pro functionality;
- allowing users to save and revisit reports;
- preventing misuse of the service; and
- maintaining service security.

Lawful Basis

Depending on the processing activity:

Contract — where necessary to provide the requested account-based or paid service.

Legitimate interests — where necessary for service security, abuse prevention and protection of the service.

Legal obligation — where processing is required by law.

Where a particular processing activity has a different purpose, its lawful basis must be assessed separately.

Retention

Unsaved searches: Not retained as user search records.

Saved reports: Retained while the user chooses to keep them, subject to account deletion and applicable legal/security retention requirements.

Report timestamp: Stored with the saved report where required to identify when the report was generated.

Data Category

Hidden History only processes profile information actually collected by the production application.

Hidden History does not intentionally collect detailed personal profiles for advertising, behavioural targeting or commercial profiling.

Profile information may be processed for:

Lawful Basis

Depending on the processing:

- contract;

6. ADVERT CONTENT AND INFORMATION CONTAINED IN SUBMITTED MATERIAL

Where a user submits vehicle advert material for analysis, Hidden History may process the content necessary to perform that analysis.

Advert content may contain personal information belonging to the seller or another person.

Hidden History does not intentionally request unnecessary personal information from adverts.

Purposes

Advert content is processed to:

- analyse vehicle advertisements;
- identify potentially suspicious claims or wording;
- identify inconsistencies;
- provide the requested vehicle analysis; and
- provide Pro analysis where applicable.

Third-Party Analysis

For Pro advert analysis, Hidden History may send relevant analysis information, raw advert content and vehicle information to Gemini for analysis.

Hidden History does not intentionally send account or unrelated personal information to Gemini.

Where personal information appears within an advert submitted by a user, Hidden History cannot guarantee that the advert itself contains no personal information.

Retention

Advert material is not retained as a separate permanent advertising database by Hidden History.

Where relevant information forms part of a saved report, it may remain associated with that saved report until the user deletes it or the account is deleted, subject to lawful retention requirements.

9. AFFILIATE AND PARTS-LINK DATA

Current Functionality

Hidden History may provide links to replacement vehicle parts using the eBay API and may participate in affiliate programmes where those programmes have actually been activated.

Affiliate applications or programmes that have been rejected, remain pending or have not been activated are not treated as current live processing activities.

Purpose

Parts-link functionality is used to help users locate replacement parts relevant to vehicle MOT advisories or other vehicle-related requirements.

Data

Hidden History does not intentionally send users' personal profiles to affiliate partners for unrelated advertising purposes.

When a user follows an external affiliate or parts link, the external provider may process information according to its own privacy and tracking practices.

Lawful Basis

The processing carried out directly by Hidden History must be assessed according to the actual technical implementation.

Where an external provider independently processes information after the user leaves Hidden History, that processing is governed by the provider's own terms and privacy information.

19. AUTOMATED ANALYSIS

Hidden History uses automated processing to analyse vehicle and advert information.

The purpose is to provide vehicle-related information and risk indicators.

The current service is not designed to make decisions about individuals based on personal characteristics or to profile users for advertising.

Automated analysis must remain explainable at an appropriate level and must not be represented as establishing legal fact merely because an automated system produced an outcome.

Where future functionality creates legal or similarly significant automated decisions concerning individuals, a separate assessment must be carried out.
