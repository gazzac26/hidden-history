# HIDDEN HISTORY
# AFFILIATE DISCLOSURE

Status: PRE-BETA / WORKING DRAFT

---

## 1. WHY THIS NOTICE EXISTS

Hidden History may provide links to third-party suppliers of vehicle
parts and related products.

Some links may be affiliate links.

---

## 2. HOW AFFILIATE LINKS WORK

If a user follows an affiliate link and subsequently purchases a
qualifying product or service, Hidden History may receive a commission
from the relevant partner.

The user does not necessarily pay an additional amount because a link
is an affiliate link.

---

## 3. CURRENT AFFILIATE FUNCTIONALITY

Hidden History may display parts-related links alongside relevant MOT
advisories or failures.

The purpose is to help users locate potential replacement parts.

Affiliate links may be available to both free and paid users.

## 6. VEHICLE DATA IS NOT DETERMINED BY AFFILIATE PARTNERS

## 7. NO GUARANTEE OF PART COMPATIBILITY

A link to a part or supplier does not guarantee that the product is
suitable for a particular vehicle.

Users must verify compatibility before purchase.

---

## 8. THIRD-PARTY PURCHASES

Purchases made through affiliate partners are transactions between the
user and the relevant third-party supplier.

The supplier's own:

- Terms
- Privacy policy
- Returns policy
- Warranty
- Delivery policy

will apply to those transactions.
