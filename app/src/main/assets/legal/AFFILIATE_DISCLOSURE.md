## 1. PURPOSE

Hidden History may provide links to third-party vehicle-parts suppliers or other relevant commercial services.

Where a link is an affiliate link, Hidden History may receive a commission if the user follows the link and completes a qualifying transaction.

The purpose of this disclosure is to make that commercial relationship clear.

---

## 2. CURRENT FUNCTIONALITY

The current Hidden History service may display vehicle-parts links alongside relevant MOT failures or advisories.

The purpose is to help users locate potential replacement parts or related products.

Affiliate links do not change the underlying vehicle data, MOT result or Hidden History analysis.

---

## 3. CURRENT AFFILIATE POSITION

eBay is the current third-party service associated with vehicle-parts sourcing functionality.

Only the affiliate programme(s) actually enabled and commercially approved for the released application should be described to users as active.

If an eBay affiliate arrangement is not active in a particular production build, the interface must not imply that a commission is being earned from that link.

---

## 4. COMMISSION

Where an affiliate arrangement is active, Hidden History may receive a commission from a qualifying purchase or other qualifying action.

The existence of an affiliate relationship does not normally increase the price paid by the user, although the third-party supplier controls its own pricing.

---

## 5. NO EFFECT ON VEHICLE ANALYSIS

Affiliate relationships do not determine:

- Vehicle identity
- DVLA information
- DVSA/MOT information
- MOT failures
- MOT advisories
- Hidden History risk findings
- Advert-analysis findings
- Vehicle suitability
- Whether a repair is required

Commercial relationships must not be allowed to influence the factual vehicle data or the analytical result.

---

## 6. NO GUARANTEE OF PART COMPATIBILITY

A linked product is not automatically guaranteed to be suitable for the user's vehicle.

Users should verify:

- Vehicle compatibility
- Correct part number
- Specification
- Fitment
- Availability
- Price
- Returns/warranty arrangements

before purchase.

---

## 7. THIRD-PARTY PURCHASES

A purchase made from an affiliate supplier is a transaction between the user and that supplier.

The supplier's own terms, privacy policy, returns arrangements, warranty, delivery arrangements and consumer obligations apply to that transaction.

Hidden History is not the seller of a third-party part merely because it provides a link.

---

## 8. DISCLOSURE PLACEMENT

Affiliate disclosures must be clear, understandable and sufficiently close to the relevant commercial link or interface.

A user should not have to search through unrelated legal documents to understand that a particular link may generate commission.

---

## 9. FAIR PRESENTATION

Affiliate links must not be presented in a way that falsely implies:

- Government endorsement
- Manufacturer endorsement
- Mechanical certification
- Guaranteed compatibility
- Guaranteed repair necessity
- Independent ranking where a commercial relationship materially affects the presentation

If multiple products are displayed, any ordering or presentation must have a genuine, documented product or user-service rationale and must not be falsely described as independent.

---

## 10. TRACKING AND PRIVACY

Affiliate-link functionality must not introduce undisclosed tracking.

Any storage/access technology, identifier or tracking mechanism used by an affiliate programme must be assessed against the applicable privacy and PECR requirements before implementation.

Hidden History currently has no general-purpose analytics or advertising system.

---

## 11. FUTURE PARTNERS

Additional affiliate partners may be introduced in future.

A partner must not be described as active until:

1. The commercial arrangement is actually approved;
2. The technical integration is live;
3. The relevant disclosure is implemented; and
4. The Affiliate Partner Register has been updated.
