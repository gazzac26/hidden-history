- General support questions

- Payment/refund concerns
- Security concerns

SovereignSoftwareLtd@gmail.com

---

## 3. DATA-PROTECTION COMPLAINTS

Hidden History maintains a process for data-protection complaints.

A user does not need to use legal terminology to raise a complaint.

A data-protection complaint may concern, for example:

- How personal information was collected or used
- A privacy request
- Account deletion
- Accuracy of personal information
- Disclosure of personal information
- Retention or deletion

---

## 4. COMPLAINT INFORMATION

Where relevant, users should provide:

- Name
- Account email address
- Vehicle registration
- Description of the issue
- Relevant date/time
- Screenshots or other evidence
- Purchase information where relevant

Users should not send unnecessary sensitive personal information.

---

## 5. RECEIPT AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

Hidden History will record the date a complaint is received.

For data-protection complaints, receipt must be acknowledged within **30 days**.

Where a complaint can be investigated and resolved within that period, the acknowledgement and outcome may be provided together.

---

## 6. INVESTIGATION

The investigation should identify:

- What happened
- Which system or process was involved
- Whether the issue concerns Hidden History or an external source
- What information is relevant
- What corrective action is available
- Whether any legal or data-protection obligations apply

---

## 7. OUTCOME

Hidden History will communicate the outcome without unjustifiable or excessive delay after the investigation is completed.

Where the complaint cannot be upheld, the response should explain the position clearly.

Where the complaint identifies an error, Hidden History should take appropriate corrective action.

---

## 8. VEHICLE DATA CORRECTION

Where the complaint concerns official vehicle information, Hidden History will distinguish between:

1. An error in the external source record; and
2. An error in Hidden History's retrieval, processing, mapping or display.

See CONTENT_AND_DATA_CORRECTION_PROCEDURE.md and VEHICLE_DATA_CORRECTION_AND_SOURCE_DISPUTE_POLICY.md.

## 10. ESCALATION

A matter should be escalated internally where it involves:

- A significant data-protection issue
- A security incident
- A suspected personal-data breach
- A complex legal dispute
- A repeated or unresolved complaint
- A regulator or law-enforcement contact

As the launch operator is currently an individual, Gary Lee Chantler is responsible for final internal escalation and decision-making.

External legal advice may be obtained where appropriate.

---

## 11. ICO

Users may complain to the Information Commission where they believe Hidden History has not complied with data-protection law.

Hidden History's process is intended to give users a clear opportunity to raise the matter directly first.

---

## 12. RECORD KEEPING

Complaint records should contain only information reasonably necessary to:

- Handle the complaint
- Demonstrate compliance
- Identify recurring problems
- Defend legal claims where necessary
