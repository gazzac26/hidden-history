1. PURPOSE

This Acceptable Use Policy explains how Hidden History must be used.

Users must use Hidden History lawfully, responsibly and in accordance
with these Terms, the Privacy Policy and any other applicable Hidden
History policies.

The policy is intended to protect:

- Users and their accounts
- Vehicle-data services
- Hidden History infrastructure
- Third-party services and data sources
- The availability and reliability of Hidden History
- The integrity of Pro access and paid functionality

---

2. PROHIBITED USE

Users must not:

- Attempt to bypass, defeat or manipulate Pro access controls.
- Attempt to obtain paid functionality without the required entitlement
  or purchase.
- Attempt to access restricted application programming interfaces
  (APIs), endpoints, databases or services without authorisation.
- Attempt to manipulate authentication, authorisation or account-access
  mechanisms.
- Use another person's account or credentials without authorisation.
- Use stolen, compromised or fraudulently obtained credentials.
- Attempt to access another user's account, saved reports or data.
- Attempt to compromise, damage or interfere with Hidden History
  infrastructure.
- Abuse, overload or deliberately disrupt vehicle-data APIs or other
  third-party services used by Hidden History.
- Circumvent technical restrictions, access controls, rate limits or
  other protective mechanisms.
- Scrape, extract, reproduce or systematically collect Hidden History
  data in a manner prohibited by applicable law, contractual terms or
  technical restrictions.
- Introduce viruses, malware, malicious code or other harmful material.
- Conduct or attempt denial-of-service or distributed
  denial-of-service activity.
- Interfere with the availability, integrity or normal operation of the
  service.
- Attempt to discover, exploit or abuse security vulnerabilities except
  where expressly authorised or reported through an applicable
  responsible-disclosure route.
- Use Hidden History to facilitate fraud, unlawful access, cybercrime or
  other unlawful activity.
- Use Hidden History in a manner that deliberately interferes with
  another user's lawful use of the service.
- Attempt to circumvent any other technical or contractual restriction
  designed to protect Hidden History, its users or its service
  providers.

---

3. AUTOMATED ACCESS

Automated requests, bots, scripts, crawlers or other automated systems
must not be used to:

- Circumvent access controls
- Circumvent rate limits
- Obtain Pro functionality without entitlement
- Abuse vehicle-data APIs
- Overload or disrupt Hidden History
- Systematically scrape or extract Hidden History data
- Circumvent restrictions imposed by Hidden History or its service
  providers

Automated access may only be used where it is expressly authorised by
Hidden History or is otherwise clearly permitted by the applicable
service terms.

---

4. PRO ACCESS

Pro functionality is controlled by Hidden History's server-side
authorisation and entitlement systems.

Users must not attempt to obtain Pro functionality by:

- Manipulating application requests
- Modifying or replaying requests for an unauthorised purpose
- Altering entitlement information
- Circumventing purchase verification
- Manipulating application state
- Selecting or calling restricted endpoints directly
- Exploiting technical vulnerabilities
- Using another user's Pro entitlement
- Sharing credentials for the purpose of circumventing payment or
  access restrictions

Any legitimate technical issue affecting a Pro entitlement should be
reported to Hidden History rather than bypassed.

---

5. VEHICLE-DATA SERVICES

Hidden History relies on external vehicle-data services and APIs.

Users must not deliberately abuse those services through Hidden History,
including by generating excessive, automated or unauthorised requests
or by attempting to circumvent restrictions imposed by the relevant
data provider.

Vehicle-data services must only be used through the functionality
provided by Hidden History and for lawful purposes.

---

6. ACCOUNT AND CREDENTIAL SECURITY

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their
account credentials and must take reasonable steps to prevent
unauthorised access to their account.

Users must not:

- Share credentials where doing so would compromise account security or
  circumvent access restrictions.
- Permit another person to use their account to obtain functionality
  they are not entitled to access.
- Attempt to obtain another person's credentials.
- Attempt to impersonate another user.
- Use credentials known or reasonably suspected to have been obtained
  without authorisation.

If a user believes their account has been compromised, they should
contact Hidden History promptly.

---

7. SECURITY TESTING AND VULNERABILITY REPORTING

Users must not conduct security testing, penetration testing,
vulnerability exploitation or other intrusive security activity against
Hidden History without prior express authorisation.

Nothing in this section prevents a user from reporting a suspected
security vulnerability to Hidden History.

Reports concerning suspected security issues should be sent to:

SovereignSoftwareLtd@gmail.com

Reports should contain sufficient information for Hidden History to
understand and investigate the issue without unnecessarily exposing
personal data or security credentials.

Hidden History must not be used for unlawful purposes.

This includes using the service to facilitate or support:

Users remain responsible for their own actions when using Hidden
History.
