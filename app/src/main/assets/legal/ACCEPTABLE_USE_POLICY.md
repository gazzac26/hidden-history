## 1. PURPOSE

This policy explains acceptable use of Hidden History.

Users must use the service lawfully and responsibly.

## 2. USERS MUST NOT

Users must not:

- Attempt to bypass Pro access controls
- Attempt to access restricted endpoints without entitlement
- Attempt to manipulate authentication
- Attempt to compromise Hidden History infrastructure
- Abuse vehicle-data APIs
- Circumvent technical restrictions
- Scrape data in violation of applicable terms
- Interfere with service availability
- Introduce malicious code
- Attempt unauthorised access to accounts
- Use stolen credentials
- Use Hidden History for unlawful purposes
