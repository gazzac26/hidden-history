## 6. DELIVERY OF THE PRO SEARCH

A Pro Search is intended to provide the relevant vehicle-search result
after successful payment and authentication.

The search depends upon the availability of the underlying vehicle-data
services.

Hidden History cannot guarantee that every requested vehicle will have
complete data available.

---

## 7. VEHICLE DATA

Pro Search results may include information such as:

- Vehicle identity
- Registration information
- Technical vehicle information
- MOT history
- MOT test results
- MOT advisories
- MOT failures
- Other information returned by connected data sources

The exact information available may vary between vehicles.

---

## 8. ANALYSIS

Where analysis is included within a Pro Search, the analysis is intended
to assist the user's vehicle research.

It is not a guarantee that a vehicle:

- Is safe
- Is mechanically sound
- Is free from defects
- Is free from accident damage
- Has accurate mileage
- Has no finance
- Has not been stolen
- Has not previously been damaged
- Has no insurance history
- Is suitable for purchase

The separate Vehicle Data and Analysis Disclaimer applies.

---

## 9. MOT INFORMATION

MOT information displayed within a Pro Search is historical information
provided by the connected MOT data service.

A passed MOT does not guarantee the vehicle's current mechanical
condition.

Where available, MOT failures and advisories may be displayed.

Users should review individual MOT results rather than relying solely
on a current MOT status.
