14. LEGAL DOCUMENT STATUS DEFINITIONS

The following status meanings apply throughout this register.

PRODUCTION

The document reflects the current Hidden History service and is suitable
for its intended production purpose, subject to any stated publication
or implementation step.

INTERNAL

The document is an internal governance, compliance, operational or
control document and is not ordinarily presented to users.

PRODUCTION — PUBLICATION REQUIRED

The document has been prepared for production but still requires its
final public publication/access mechanism before launch.

NOT CURRENTLY APPLICABLE

The document relates to functionality or processing that does not exist
in the current Hidden History application.

FUTURE-USE CONTROLLED DOCUMENT

The document has been retained for planned future functionality but must
not be treated as active until the relevant functionality and legal
arrangements exist.
