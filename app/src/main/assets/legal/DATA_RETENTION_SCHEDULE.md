---

## 1. PURPOSE

This document establishes the intended retention periods for information
processed by Hidden History.

The final values must be confirmed against the production architecture
before beta release.

---

## 2. RETENTION PRINCIPLE

Information should not be retained for longer than reasonably necessary
for the purpose for which it is processed, subject to legal obligations.

## 6. LEGAL HOLDS

Information may be retained beyond normal periods where reasonably
necessary for:

- Legal claims
- Fraud prevention
- Security investigations
- Regulatory obligations
- Accounting obligations

## 7. REVIEW

- New data is collected
- A new purpose is introduced
- A provider changes
- Storage architecture changes
- Legal requirements change
