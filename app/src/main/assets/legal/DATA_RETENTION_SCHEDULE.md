## 1. PURPOSE

This schedule defines the current retention position for Hidden History.

Retention is based on purpose and lawful necessity rather than a universal arbitrary period.

---

## 2. CORE PRINCIPLE

Hidden History does not retain information merely because it might become useful later.

Information should be:

- Adequate
- Relevant
- Limited to what is necessary
- Kept only for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose
- Securely deleted when no longer required

---

## 3. CURRENT RETENTION SCHEDULE

| Category | Current position |
|---|---|
| Unsaved vehicle searches | Not retained as user search history |
| Account/profile data | While account is active, then deleted subject to lawful retention |
| Saved vehicle reports | Until user deletion/account deletion, subject to lawful retention |
| Report-generation timestamp | With the saved report while it is retained |
| Generated PDFs | Not stored by Hidden History |
| Advert text used for Pro analysis | Processed for the relevant Pro analysis; not retained as an unsaved search record |
| Pro entitlement/purchase information | Only as required for entitlement, support, accounting or legal purposes; Google controls payment processing |
| Security/abuse records | Only as reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, investigation or legal purposes |
| Support records | Only as reasonably necessary to resolve the matter and demonstrate/defend compliance |
| Data-protection request records | Only as reasonably necessary to evidence and manage the request |
| Backup copies | Provider-dependent backup lifecycle; deleted records should be excluded from normal operational use and removed through the backup lifecycle |

---

## 4. UNSAVED SEARCHES

A user can search for a vehicle without creating a saved report.

The search is processed to obtain and display the requested information but is not retained as a persistent search history.

Necessary temporary technical/security processing may occur where required for operation or security.

---

## 5. SAVED REPORTS

When a user chooses to save a report, the report becomes an account-linked record.

The report remains available until:

- The user deletes it;
- The user deletes the account; or
- A lawful retention requirement requires a limited period of continued retention.

---

## 6. ACCOUNT DELETION

Account deletion should remove associated account-linked personal information and saved reports from live systems, subject only to lawful retention.

See ACCOUNT_DELETION_AND_DATA_RETENTION.md.

---

## 7. PAYMENT INFORMATION

Google Play Billing handles payment processing for Pro purchases.

Hidden History does not intentionally store users' full payment-card details.

Hidden History may receive or retain limited transaction/entitlement information necessary to provide Pro access, support users, reconcile transactions or comply with legal obligations.

---

## 8. SECURITY AND TECHNICAL RECORDS

Technical/security records should be limited to what is necessary.

They may include information needed to:

- Detect abuse
- Protect accounts
- Investigate incidents
- Maintain system security
- Diagnose technical failures
- Defend legal claims where necessary

They must not become a general behavioural-tracking system.

---

## 9. BACKUPS

Backups may contain information that has been deleted from live systems until the relevant backup cycle expires.

Where practical, deleted information should be excluded from operational restoration and permanently removed through the provider's backup lifecycle.

Actual provider retention must be recorded in the operational provider register where material.

---

## 10. LEGAL RETENTION

Where a law requires information to be retained, Hidden History should:

- Identify the legal requirement;
- Retain only what is necessary;
- Restrict access;
- Protect the information;
- Delete it when the legal requirement ends.

---

## 11. REVIEW TRIGGERS

Review this schedule when:

- Data categories change
- Saved-report behaviour changes
- New providers are introduced
- New legal retention obligations arise
- Payment processing changes
- Security logging changes
- MIB/MIAFTR becomes operational
- The legal operator changes
