1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes Hidden History's approach to age restrictions, child safety and the protection of children in connection with the Hidden History service.

Hidden History is an adult-only vehicle information, analysis and purchasing-assistance service.

The current service is not designed for children and Hidden History does not intentionally target children.

---

2. MINIMUM AGE

The minimum user age for Hidden History is:

18 years old.

Users must be aged 18 or over to create or use a Hidden History account.

This age requirement must be reflected consistently across:

- the application;
- account creation;
- Terms and Conditions;
- Privacy Policy;
- relevant onboarding and age declarations;
- Google Play target-audience and content declarations; and
- other public-facing service information where applicable.

Hidden History does not intentionally provide its current service to users under 18.

---

3. AGE ASSURANCE AND AGE INFORMATION

Hidden History will use an age restriction appropriate to the risks and functionality of the service.

Hidden History will not collect detailed age information merely because it could do so.

Where an age declaration or other age-assurance mechanism is used, Hidden History will apply data-minimisation principles and will only collect or retain information necessary for the legitimate purpose of establishing or enforcing the minimum age requirement.

Hidden History will not introduce intrusive age-assurance technology unless a future assessment establishes that it is necessary and proportionate.

Any future age-assurance system involving additional personal information will be subject to a privacy and security review before implementation.

The ICO's Children's Code takes a risk-based approach to age assurance and requires the level of certainty used to be appropriate to the risks arising from the service.

---

4. UNDER-AGE ACCOUNTS

If Hidden History becomes aware, or has reasonable grounds to believe, that an account has been created or used by a person under 18:

- access may be restricted or suspended;
- the account may be reviewed;
- the user may be asked to cease using the service;
- personal information associated with the account will be assessed for deletion;
- information will be deleted where there is no lawful reason to retain it; and
- the matter may be escalated where necessary for legal, safety or security reasons.

Hidden History will not knowingly continue providing an adult-only account to a user who is below the minimum age.

---

5. CHILDREN'S PERSONAL DATA

Hidden History does not intentionally seek to collect children's personal information.

Users must not submit unnecessary personal information about children through Hidden History.

Where information relating to a child is included unintentionally in material submitted to Hidden History, such as vehicle advert text, Hidden History will handle that information in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection requirements.

Hidden History does not use children's information for:

- advertising;
- behavioural profiling;
- marketing;
- sale to third parties; or
- unrelated commercial purposes.

---

6. USER-GENERATED CONTENT

The current Hidden History service does not provide:

- community forums;
- public discussion areas;
- messaging;
- marketplace listings;
- public user profiles;
- social posting; or
- other general user-generated-content functionality.

Accordingly, the current service does not provide children with social interaction features or public communication facilities.

If user-generated-content functionality is introduced in the future, this policy will be reviewed before release.

---

7. CHILD SAFETY REPORTING

Child-safety concerns relating to Hidden History should be reported to:

SovereignSoftwareLtd@gmail.com

Reports may include concerns about:

- an under-age account;
- suspected child exploitation or abuse;
- inappropriate contact involving a child;
- child-related personal information;
- misuse of Hidden History by a minor; or
- other serious child-safety concerns connected with the service.

Hidden History will assess reports appropriately and may take action including restricting access, deleting information, preserving relevant evidence where legally necessary, or referring matters to an appropriate authority where required.

---

8. SAFETY AND PERSONAL INFORMATION

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information relating to children.

Where Hidden History encounters information that appears to create a serious child-safety or privacy concern, it may take reasonable steps to protect affected individuals and the service.

Any retention of information for safety, security or legal purposes will be limited to what is necessary and handled in accordance with the Privacy Policy and applicable law.

---

9. FUTURE FEATURES

Any future introduction of:

- messaging;
- community functionality;
- public profiles;
- marketplace functionality;
- social features;
- user-generated content; or
- other functionality likely to increase interaction between users

will require a separate child-safety and privacy assessment before release.

The assessment will consider, where applicable:

- whether children are likely to access the feature;
- whether the 18+ restriction remains appropriate;
- age-assurance requirements;
- risks to children;
- moderation requirements;
- reporting mechanisms;
- personal-data processing;
- privacy-by-design requirements;
- applicable Online Safety Act obligations;
- applicable Children's Code requirements; and
- Google Play policy requirements.

No future feature will be assumed to be covered by this policy without reviewing whether its actual operation changes the relevant risks or legal obligations.

---

10. GOOGLE PLAY COMPLIANCE

Hidden History's Google Play declarations must accurately reflect its intended audience and actual functionality.

The application's age restriction, target-audience declarations, content rating and Data Safety information must remain consistent with the production application.

Google Play requires developers to provide accurate target-audience and related policy information and to update those declarations where the application changes.

Where Hidden History is configured as an 18+ service, Hidden History will maintain consistency between the application's age restriction and its Google Play configuration.

---

This Child Safety and Age Policy should be read together with that Privacy Policy, particularly where information concerning a child is submitted unintentionally.

Hidden History applies data-minimisation and privacy-by-design principles to information relating to children.
