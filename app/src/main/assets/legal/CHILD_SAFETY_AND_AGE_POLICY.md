## 4. UNDER-AGE ACCOUNTS

If Hidden History becomes aware that an account has been created by a
person below the permitted age:

- Review the account.

- Remove information where appropriate.

Hidden History does not intentionally seek to collect children's
personal information beyond what is necessary for a lawful service.

Users should not submit unnecessary information about children.
