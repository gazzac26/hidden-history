4. PROCESSING ACTIVITY — VEHICLE SEARCH

Purpose

- Provide vehicle searches requested by users.
- Retrieve vehicle information.
- Cross-check available vehicle information.
- Display vehicle information.
- Provide vehicle-related analysis.

Categories of Individuals

Primarily:

- Hidden History users requesting vehicle information.

Vehicle records may also contain information relating to other individuals indirectly, depending on the nature of the source information.

Personal Data / Information

Depending on the requested service:

- Vehicle registration number.
- Vehicle information obtained from external sources.
- MOT information where applicable.
- Vehicle analysis information.
- Search timestamp where generated or required.
- Account association where the search forms part of an account-based service.

Sources

- DVLA vehicle information services.
- DVSA / MOT information services.

Lawful Basis

Depending on the particular processing:

- Contract — where necessary to provide the requested service.
- Legitimate interests — where necessary for security, abuse prevention or service protection.
- Legal obligation — where applicable.

Retention

Normal unsaved vehicle searches are not retained as user search-history records.

Where a user chooses to save a report, the information forming part of that report may be retained in the user's account until deletion or account deletion, subject to lawful retention requirements.

Purpose

- Identify inconsistencies.
- Provide enhanced Pro analysis.

Categories of Individuals

- Hidden History users submitting adverts.
- Individuals whose personal information may incidentally appear within submitted advert material.

Personal Data / Information

Advert analysis may involve:

- Raw advert content submitted by the user.
- Vehicle information.
- Hidden History's own advert-analysis results.
- Information necessary to produce the requested analysis.

Third-Party Analysis Service

Gemini is used as an analysis service for Pro advert analysis.

Hidden History may send relevant advert-analysis information, including:

- the submitted advert;
- vehicle information; and
- Hidden History analysis results or related structured information.

Advert content may nevertheless contain personal information belonging to a seller or another person, because Hidden History does not control the original content supplied by the user.

Lawful Basis

Contract — where necessary to provide the requested paid Pro analysis.

Retention

Advert content is not intentionally maintained as a separate permanent advertising database.

Where advert information forms part of a saved report, it may remain within that report until deletion or account deletion, subject to lawful retention requirements.

16. AUTOMATED PROCESSING

Hidden History uses automated processing for vehicle and advert analysis.

The purpose is to provide vehicle-related information, analysis and risk indicators.

The current service does not use automated processing to profile users for advertising or behavioural targeting.

Automated analysis does not, by itself, establish a legal fact or guarantee that a vehicle or advert is fraudulent, unsafe or otherwise defective.

If future functionality involves solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on individuals, the processing must be separately assessed before implementation.
