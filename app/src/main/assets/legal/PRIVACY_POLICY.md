3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Hidden History collects and processes only information required to provide, secure and support the service.

Account information

Where you create a Hidden History account, we may process:

- Email address
- Account identifier
- Authentication information
- Profile information voluntarily provided by you
- Pro entitlement or purchase-related information required to provide paid functionality

Your account information is used to provide authentication and account functionality.

Vehicle-search information

When you conduct a vehicle search, Hidden History processes information required to obtain and display the requested vehicle information.

This may include:

- Vehicle registration number
- Vehicle information returned by official vehicle-data sources
- MOT information
- MOT test results
- Recorded mileage
- MOT advisories
- MOT failures

A vehicle search that is not saved as a report is not retained as a user's search history by Hidden History.

Saved vehicle reports

If you choose to save a report, Hidden History stores the report information associated with your account so that you can:

- View the report again
- Download the report
- Share the report

A saved report may contain:

- Vehicle information
- Vehicle search results
- MOT information
- Advertisement analysis information
- Report-generation information
- The date and time at which the report was generated

Technical and security information

Hidden History may process limited technical or security information where necessary to operate and protect the service.

This may include information generated by the application, authentication infrastructure or hosting infrastructure where required for security, reliability, error handling or prevention of abuse.

Hidden History does not operate a separate behavioural analytics system.

Information contained in vehicle advertisements

Where you choose to submit a vehicle advertisement for analysis, the advertisement itself is processed as part of the analysis.

An advertisement may contain personal information that was placed in it by its author or seller.

Hidden History does not intentionally request or collect personal information about individuals through vehicle advertisements.

5. VEHICLE REGISTRATION NUMBERS

A vehicle registration number is required to retrieve vehicle information through Hidden History.

When you enter a registration number, it is processed to perform the requested vehicle search.

The registration number may be sent to the relevant vehicle-data service where necessary to retrieve the requested information.

Hidden History does not retain an unsaved vehicle search as a permanent user search history.

Where you choose to save a report, the relevant vehicle information, including the registration number where included in the report, may form part of that saved report.

If you delete the saved report, the report is deleted from the live Hidden History system, subject to information that must lawfully be retained.

7. OFFICIAL VEHICLE DATA SOURCES

Hidden History currently obtains vehicle information from official vehicle-data services, including:

- DVLA vehicle information
- DVSA/MOT information

These sources provide information about vehicles and MOT testing.

Hidden History retrieves, processes, cross-checks and presents information made available by these sources.

Hidden History does not control the underlying government databases.

Information supplied by an external source may contain omissions, delays or inaccuracies.

Where an error originates from an external source, Hidden History may be unable to change the underlying government record.

Hidden History may analyse vehicle information and vehicle advertisements to assist users with vehicle research.

11. DATA RETENTION

Hidden History follows a data-minimisation approach to retention.

Unsaved searches

Unsaved vehicle searches are not retained as a user's permanent search history.

Saved reports

A vehicle report is stored when the user chooses to save it.

The saved report remains available to the user until the user deletes it, the account is deleted, or continued availability is prevented by technical, legal or security circumstances.

Account information

Account information is retained while the account is active so that the account and associated services can operate.

When the user requests account deletion, ordinary account-linked information and saved reports are deleted from the live Hidden History system, subject to information that must lawfully be retained.

Legally required information

Where Hidden History is legally required to retain information, that information will be retained only for the period required and will be subject to appropriate access restrictions and security measures.

Security information

Limited information may need to be retained where necessary to investigate security incidents, prevent abuse, protect the service or comply with legal obligations.

Hidden History does not retain information simply because it might be useful in the future.

12. SAVED REPORTS AND PDF FILES

When you save a vehicle report, the report information is stored in your Hidden History account.

The saved report may include the date and time at which the report was generated.

When you choose to download or share a report, Hidden History generates a PDF within the application.

The generated PDF contains the relevant report information and timestamp.

Hidden History does not maintain a separate permanent archive of user-generated PDF files.

The PDF is generated for the user to download or share.

If you delete a saved report, the saved report is deleted from the live Hidden History system, subject to any lawful retention requirement.
