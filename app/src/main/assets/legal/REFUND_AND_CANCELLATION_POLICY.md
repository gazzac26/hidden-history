## 6. REFUNDS

A refund may be considered where:

- A payment was taken incorrectly
- A paid service was not supplied
- A technical failure prevented delivery
- The service supplied was materially different from what was
  purchased
- A refund is otherwise required by applicable law
- Hidden History determines that a refund is appropriate under its
  final refund rules
