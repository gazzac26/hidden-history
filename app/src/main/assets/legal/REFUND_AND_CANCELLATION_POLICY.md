1. PURPOSE

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how Hidden History handles
refunds, cancellation and withdrawal requests relating to paid Pro
services.

The policy applies to the Pro functionality actually offered to the
consumer at the time of purchase.

It must be read together with:

- Hidden History Terms and Conditions
- Pro Search Purchase Terms
- Consumer Purchase and Digital Service Notice
- Vehicle Data and Analysis Disclaimer
- Privacy Policy

Nothing in this policy removes or limits any consumer right or remedy
that cannot lawfully be excluded.

---

2. CURRENT PAID SERVICE

The current paid Hidden History service is:

Pro vehicle search and/or enhanced Pro vehicle analysis

The current commercial model is:

- One-off purchase
- No recurring subscription
- No automatic renewal
- No subscription cancellation cycle

The current intended consumer price range is:

£2.99–£4.99 per Pro report

The actual price charged is the price displayed to the consumer before
the purchase is completed.

---

3. FREE SERVICES

Free vehicle searches and free vehicle/advert analysis do not require
payment.

A refund does not arise for a free service because no purchase has been
made.

---

4. PRO SEARCH PURCHASES

A Pro Search is supplied as a paid digital service/product through the
Hidden History application.

Before purchase, the consumer must be shown sufficient information to
understand:

- What they are purchasing
- The applicable price
- The currency
- What the Pro service provides
- How the service is supplied
- Any material limitations
- Applicable cancellation/withdrawal information
- Applicable refund information

The production purchase flow must remain consistent with these terms.

---

5. PAYMENT PROVIDER

Pro purchases are processed through:

Google Play Billing

Google Play provides its own refund and purchase-support processes.

Consumers may contact Hidden History directly regarding an in-app
purchase that was not delivered or is not working as expected. Google
also states that developers can process refunds in accordance with their
policies and applicable law.

Hidden History does not intend to collect or store complete payment-card
details.

---

6. WHEN A REFUND MAY APPLY

A refund will be provided where required by applicable law.

A refund may also be appropriate where:

- A payment was taken incorrectly.
- The consumer was charged more than once because of a technical or
  payment error.
- The purchased Pro service was not supplied.
- A technical failure prevented reasonable delivery of the purchased
  service.
- The service supplied materially differed from the service described
  before purchase.
- Hidden History supplied the wrong product because of an error.
- A transaction was incorrectly processed.
- Hidden History determines that a refund is appropriate under this
  policy.

This list does not limit any statutory consumer right.

---

7. INCOMPLETE VEHICLE DATA

Vehicle information obtained from external data sources may be
incomplete.

For example, a vehicle record may contain some information but not
other information that a consumer expected to see.

An incomplete external vehicle record does not automatically mean that
the Pro service failed.

Where the purchased Pro service was supplied as described but an
underlying data source does not contain particular information, the
absence of that information alone does not automatically create a
refund entitlement.

However, where Hidden History cannot reasonably supply the purchased
service as described, the consumer will receive the remedy required by
applicable law and/or this policy.

---

8. THIRD-PARTY DATA OUTAGES

Hidden History relies on external vehicle-data services.

A temporary outage or failure affecting those services may prevent a
Pro Search from being completed.

Where a provider outage prevents reasonable delivery of the purchased
Pro service, Hidden History will provide an appropriate remedy.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

- Retrying the search
- Providing the affected report once the service becomes available
- Providing a replacement Pro search where appropriate
- Providing a refund

The appropriate remedy will depend on the circumstances and applicable
consumer rights.

---

9. TECHNICAL FAILURE

If a technical failure prevents the consumer from receiving the
purchased Pro service, the consumer should contact Hidden History.

Where the service can reasonably be supplied after the problem is
resolved, Hidden History may first offer a retry or replacement.

Where the purchased service cannot reasonably be supplied, an
appropriate refund or other statutory remedy will be provided.

---

10. DUPLICATE PAYMENTS

If a consumer is charged more than once because of a technical or
payment-processing error, Hidden History will investigate the
transactions against the available Google Play purchase information.

Where a duplicate charge is confirmed, the duplicate transaction will
be refunded or otherwise remedied as required.

A consumer deliberately purchasing the same Pro service more than once
is not automatically treated as having been charged in error.

---

11. ACCIDENTAL PURCHASES

Accidental purchases are assessed according to the circumstances,
applicable consumer law and the applicable Google Play refund process.

Hidden History does not intend to create a blanket contractual rule
that automatically rejects every accidental-purchase request.

Consumers should submit an accidental-purchase request promptly.

Google Play also provides processes for certain accidental or
unauthorised purchases.

---

12. CANCELLATION AND WITHDRAWAL RIGHTS

The applicable cancellation/withdrawal position depends on the legal
nature of the purchased Pro service and the way in which it is supplied.

Where applicable law provides a statutory cancellation or withdrawal
right, Hidden History will honour that right.

Where a consumer requests immediate supply of a digital service during
a statutory cancellation period, the purchase flow must contain any
express request, acknowledgement or other information required by
applicable law.

UK rules contain specific requirements concerning services supplied
during the cancellation period and digital content supplied
immediately.

The production purchase flow must therefore accurately reflect the
actual Pro delivery model.

---

13. IMMEDIATE SUPPLY

Where Hidden History begins supplying the Pro service immediately after
purchase, the application must provide any legally required information
and obtain any required consumer consent or acknowledgement before
supply begins.

Hidden History must not rely on a generic statement that purchasing a
Pro report automatically removes every cancellation or withdrawal
right.

The actual legal treatment depends on the applicable classification of
the purchased service and the manner in which it is supplied.

Google Play's current UK guidance distinguishes digital services from
digital content and describes different statutory withdrawal
treatments.

---

14. REFUND REQUESTS

Refund requests may be submitted through:

SovereignSoftwareLtd@gmail.com

Where available, consumers may also use the applicable Google Play
refund process.

Google Play currently advises UK users that refund requests can be made
through Google Play and that developers can assist with purchase issues
and process refunds where appropriate.

---

15. INFORMATION TO PROVIDE

To help Hidden History investigate a refund request, the consumer
should provide, where available:

- Hidden History account email
- Google Play order/transaction reference
- Date of purchase
- Vehicle registration, where relevant
- Description of the problem
- Reason for requesting a refund
- Relevant supporting information

Consumers should not send complete payment-card details.

Hidden History will only request information reasonably necessary to
investigate the request.

---

16. REFUND INVESTIGATION

Refund requests will be reviewed against:

- This policy
- The Pro Search Purchase Terms
- The actual product purchased
- The service actually supplied
- Available Google Play transaction information
- Any relevant technical records
- Applicable consumer law

Where additional investigation is required, Hidden History may request
reasonable further information from the consumer.

---

17. REFUND DECISION

Hidden History aims to acknowledge refund requests promptly and,
where practical, provide an initial decision within 5 business days.

This is a service target and does not restrict any statutory right.

Where a matter requires additional investigation, the consumer will be
informed that the review is continuing.

Where a refund is approved, Hidden History will initiate the refund
through the applicable payment/refund mechanism.

The time taken for the funds to reach the consumer may depend on Google
Play and the consumer's payment provider.

Google Play currently states that refund decisions commonly take between
one and four days, although timing can vary.

---

18. GOOGLE PLAY REFUNDS

Google Play has its own refund rules for purchases made through Google
Play Billing.

Those rules may depend on:

- The type of purchase
- The date of purchase
- The consumer's location
- Whether the purchase is faulty
- Whether the purchase matches its description
- Other circumstances covered by Google Play policy

Hidden History's policy does not replace Google Play's own refund
process.

Google Play currently states that UK consumers may receive refunds for
purchases that are faulty or do not match their description, and that
developers may also assist with eligible purchase problems.

---

19. CHARGEBACKS AND PAYMENT DISPUTES

Consumers should contact Hidden History where they believe a payment
has been taken incorrectly.

Nothing in this policy prevents a consumer from using any rights or
processes available through their payment provider or under applicable
law.

Hidden History may investigate a disputed transaction where appropriate.

---

20. NO AUTOMATIC REFUND FOR A VEHICLE NOT BEING PURCHASED

A Pro report is a research and information service.

A consumer deciding not to purchase the vehicle after receiving a Pro
report does not, by itself, mean that the service was not supplied.

Similarly, a report identifying risks or concerns does not constitute a
failure of the service merely because the findings are unfavourable to
the vehicle.

The service must nevertheless match its description and applicable
consumer-law requirements.

---

21. NO AUTOMATIC REFUND FOR AN UNFAVOURABLE RESULT

Hidden History does not guarantee that a Pro report will:

- Recommend purchasing a vehicle
- Produce a particular risk score
- Identify a particular issue
- Confirm that a vehicle is safe
- Confirm that a vehicle is mechanically sound
- Confirm that a vehicle is free from defects

The purpose of the Pro service is to provide the analysis and
information described at the time of purchase.

An unfavourable result does not automatically create a refund
entitlement.

This does not affect statutory consumer rights where the service fails
to meet the required legal standard.

---

22. REPORT DELIVERY

Where the purchased Pro service includes a report, the report is
considered supplied when the applicable report has been successfully
generated and made available to the consumer through the application.

Where a report-generation failure prevents the consumer from receiving
the purchased report, the matter will be handled under the technical
failure and refund provisions of this policy.

---

23. SAVED REPORTS

Where the application permits the Pro report to be saved, the saved
report is governed by the Hidden History data-retention and account
rules.

Deleting a saved report does not itself create a refund entitlement
where the Pro service was already correctly supplied.

---

24. PDF REPORTS

Where the application generates a PDF for download or sharing, the PDF
is generated for the consumer's use.

Hidden History does not permanently store the generated PDF as part of
its ordinary report-storage system.

A problem with a consumer's locally stored copy does not automatically
mean that the original Pro service was not supplied.

Where the report itself was not correctly generated or supplied, the
consumer may request an appropriate remedy.

---

25. REFUNDS AND AFFILIATE LINKS

Affiliate links that may appear in a Pro report do not affect the
consumer's purchase of the Pro service.

An affiliate commission does not determine whether a refund is
available.

Refund decisions are based on the purchased Pro service, the
circumstances of the transaction and applicable law.

---

26. CANCELLATION OF PRO ACCESS

The current Pro service is a one-off purchase.

There is therefore no recurring Pro subscription to cancel.

Where a consumer has an applicable statutory cancellation or withdrawal
right, the cancellation/withdrawal process will operate in accordance
with that right.

Where a refund is issued after a Pro entitlement has been granted,
Hidden History may revoke the associated Pro entitlement where
appropriate and lawful.

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or attempts to waive a
consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

- Services not supplied
- Services not matching their description
- Faulty digital services
