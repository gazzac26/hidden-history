1. PURPOSE

This policy explains how Hidden History uses cookies, software development kits (SDKs), local storage and other similar technologies that may store information on, or access information from, a user's device.

Because Hidden History is primarily a mobile application, these technologies may operate through application code, SDKs, local storage or other mobile technologies rather than traditional web browser cookies.

Hidden History applies a data-minimisation and privacy-by-design approach and does not use tracking technologies for advertising, behavioural profiling or commercial surveillance.

---

2. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

Hidden History does not currently operate a general-purpose advertising, analytics or tracking-cookie system.

Where technologies are used within the application that store or access information on the user's device, Hidden History will assess their purpose and operation to determine whether they are:

- strictly necessary for the service requested by the user;
- subject to applicable consent requirements;
- processing personal data; or
- otherwise subject to applicable privacy and electronic communications requirements.

The same principles apply to mobile SDKs and other technologies that may perform functions similar to cookies.

---

3. ESSENTIAL TECHNOLOGIES

Hidden History may use technologies that are strictly necessary to operate and secure the service.

These may include technologies required to:

- authenticate users;
- maintain authenticated sessions;
- maintain essential application state;
- protect accounts and service functionality;
- prevent abuse or unauthorised access;
- support secure communication with service providers;
- maintain service reliability; and
- provide functionality specifically requested by the user.

Where a technology is strictly necessary for the service requested by the user, Hidden History may rely on the applicable PECR exemption rather than seeking consent.

Hidden History will not describe a technology as "essential" merely for convenience where it is actually being used for optional analytics, advertising, profiling or tracking.

---

4. ANALYTICS

Hidden History does not currently use third-party analytics services or analytics tracking SDKs.

There is currently:

- no Firebase Analytics;
- no Google Analytics;
- no third-party behavioural analytics platform;
- no advertising analytics platform; and
- no deliberate user-tracking analytics system.

Hidden History therefore does not currently use analytics cookies or similar technologies for measuring user behaviour across services.

If analytics functionality is introduced in the future, this policy and the Privacy Policy will be reviewed before that functionality is activated.

The review will identify, where applicable:

- the provider;
- the technology or SDK;
- the purpose;
- the information collected;
- whether personal data is involved;
- retention;
- applicable lawful basis;
- whether PECR consent is required;
- how consent and withdrawal will operate; and
- any relevant changes to the Google Play Data Safety declaration.

No analytics technology will be introduced on the assumption that it is automatically permitted merely because the information collected is described as anonymous.

---

5. ADVERTISING AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

Hidden History does not currently use advertising technologies.

The application does not currently use:

- advertising SDKs;
- personalised advertising;
- behavioural advertising;
- advertising identifiers for Hidden History advertising purposes;
- advertising tracking pixels;
- third-party advertising cookies;
- cross-service behavioural tracking; or
- user profiling for advertising.

Hidden History does not sell user information to advertisers and does not operate an advertising-funded user-tracking model.

If advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced in the future, they will be subject to a separate compliance review before activation, including assessment of applicable PECR consent requirements and UK data-protection requirements.

---

6. THIRD-PARTY SDKs

Hidden History may contain third-party SDKs or software components where they are required to provide or support legitimate application functionality.

The current production architecture includes third-party services and components associated with functions such as:

- authentication and backend services through Supabase;
- payment and purchase functionality through Google Play Billing;
- vehicle information obtained through applicable DVLA and DVSA services;
- Pro advert analysis through Gemini; and
- replacement-part links using the eBay API.

The presence of a third-party service does not, by itself, mean that Hidden History uses that service for advertising, analytics or tracking.

Hidden History remains responsible for understanding the behaviour of software components incorporated into the application, including whether those components store information on, or access information from, a user's device.

Before adding a new SDK or material software component, Hidden History should assess:

- its purpose;
- information stored or accessed;
- whether it communicates with a third party;
- whether personal data is processed;
- whether tracking occurs;
- whether PECR applies;
- whether consent is required;
- security implications;
- retention;
- relevant processor/controller responsibilities; and
- consistency with the Privacy Policy and Google Play disclosures.

Only SDKs and software components actually present in the production application should be described as being used by Hidden History.

---

7. LOCAL APPLICATION STORAGE

Hidden History may use local device storage where necessary for application functionality.

Local storage may include temporary or operational information required to:

- operate the application;
- maintain application state;
- support authentication or session functionality;
- improve reliability; or
- provide functionality requested by the user.

Local device storage does not automatically mean that information is transmitted to Hidden History or retained by Hidden History.

Where information remains solely on the user's device, it is not treated as Hidden History server-side data merely because the application created or accessed it.

---

8. WEBVIEW AND EXTERNAL SERVICES

Some Hidden History functionality may display or access web-based content or external services.

Where a website or external service sets cookies or uses similar technologies within its own service, those technologies may be controlled by that service rather than by Hidden History.

Hidden History does not claim responsibility for cookies or similar technologies independently operated by an external website.

Where Hidden History controls or materially facilitates the use of a storage or access technology, its operation will be assessed against applicable requirements.

---

9. USER CHOICE AND CONSENT

Where applicable law requires consent for a storage or access technology, Hidden History will obtain valid consent before using that technology.

Where consent is required, it must be:

- freely given;
- specific;
- informed;
- unambiguous; and
- provided through a clear affirmative action.

Users will not be required to consent to non-essential tracking as a condition of receiving functionality where that tracking is not necessary for the requested service.

Where consent is used, Hidden History will provide an appropriate means of withdrawing it.

---

10. PRIVACY BY DESIGN AND DATA MINIMISATION

Hidden History does not use cookies or similar technologies as a means of collecting information simply because it is technically possible to do so.

The service is designed around data minimisation.

Hidden History does not currently operate:

- user-behaviour tracking for advertising;
- commercial profiling;
- analytics-based user surveillance;
- advertising profiles;
- data brokerage; or
- sale of user information for unrelated commercial purposes.

Technologies will only be used where there is a legitimate operational, security, legal or service-related reason.
