## 1. PURPOSE

This procedure explains how Hidden History handles privacy and data-protection requests.

Users should be able to exercise applicable rights without unnecessary difficulty.

---

## 2. REQUEST TYPES

Requests may include:

- Access
- Rectification
- Erasure
- Restriction
- Objection
- Data portability where applicable
- Withdrawal of consent where consent is the lawful basis
- Account deletion

---

## 3. REQUEST CONTACT

Privacy requests:

SovereignSoftwareLtd@gmail.com

---

## 4. REQUEST RECEIPT

When a request is received:

1. Record the date.
2. Identify the request type.
3. Verify identity where reasonably necessary.
4. Clarify only where genuinely required.
5. Identify relevant data and systems.
6. Assess the applicable right.
7. Check for exemptions or limitations.
8. Take the required action.
9. Respond within the applicable legal timeframe.
10. Record completion.

---

## 5. IDENTITY VERIFICATION

Verification must be proportionate.

Do not collect excessive identification information.

Where the request is made through an authenticated account, existing account information may help establish identity.

---

## 6. ACCESS

For access requests:

- Search relevant Hidden History systems
- Identify personal information
- Identify processing purposes
- Identify relevant recipients
- Consider source/provider relationships
- Check applicable exemptions
- Prepare the response securely

See DSAR_PROCEDURE.md.

---

## 7. RECTIFICATION

Where personal information is inaccurate:

- Identify the disputed information
- Determine whether Hidden History controls it
- Correct Hidden History's own inaccurate records where appropriate
- Where an external source controls the underlying record, explain the position and direct the user to the source's correction route where appropriate
- Correct Hidden History processing/display errors separately

---

## 8. ERASURE

Where erasure applies:

- Identify information to delete
- Identify information that must lawfully be retained
- Delete or anonymise appropriate information
- Consider relevant processors
- Record completion

Account deletion must follow the account deletion procedure and Google Play requirements where applicable.

---

## 9. RESTRICTION

Where restriction applies, processing should be restricted while the relevant issue is investigated, subject to applicable legal conditions.

The restriction should be technically enforced where practical.

---

## 10. OBJECTION

Objections must be assessed against:

- The relevant processing activity
- Lawful basis
- Applicable statutory right
- Any overriding grounds available to Hidden History

Where legitimate interests is relied upon, the LIA must be considered.

---

## 11. DATA PORTABILITY

Where the right applies, Hidden History should provide the relevant personal information in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format where required.

Portability is not a universal right for every processing activity.

---

## 12. CONSENT WITHDRAWAL

Where processing relies on consent, the user must be able to withdraw consent.

Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

---

## 13. REQUEST LOG

Record:

- Date received
- Requester
- Verification
- Request type
- Systems searched
- Decision
- Action taken
- Date completed
- Any applicable limitation/exemption
- Relevant communications

Keep the record only as long as necessary for compliance and legal protection.

---

## 14. ESCALATION

Complex matters should be escalated to Gary Lee Chantler.

External legal/privacy advice may be obtained where appropriate.

Escalation is appropriate for:

- Complex legal exemptions
- Third-party controller disputes
- Large or unusual requests
- Suspected breaches
- Regulatory correspondence
- Repeated disputed requests

---

## 15. SECURITY

Privacy requests must be handled securely.

Personal information must not be disclosed to an unauthorised person.

---

## 16. REVIEW

Review following material changes to:

- Privacy law
- Data architecture
- User rights functionality
- Account deletion
- Processors
- Data sources
