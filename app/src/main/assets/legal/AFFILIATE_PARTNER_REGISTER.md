---

## 6. APPROVAL RULE

Before a partner is treated as active, confirm:

1. Commercial relationship exists;
2. Applicable terms have been reviewed;
3. Technical integration is live;
4. Disclosure is implemented;
5. Privacy/PECR implications have been considered;
6. The partner is recorded accurately in this register.

---

## 7. PARTNER REMOVAL

If an affiliate arrangement ends:

- Disable the relevant integration;
- Remove or update public disclosure as appropriate;
- Mark the partner inactive;
- Record the date and reason internally;
- Check that no stale affiliate links remain in production.

---

## 8. RECORD KEEPING

Keep copies or references to the applicable commercial terms, approval evidence and material changes securely.

Do not store unnecessary user personal information in this register.

---

## 9. REVIEW

Review before beta and whenever:

- A new partner is added
- A partner ends
- Commission terms change
- Link behaviour changes
- Tracking technology changes
- A partner begins receiving personal information
- The commercial presentation changes
