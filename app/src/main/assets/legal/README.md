## PURPOSE

This folder contains the legal, privacy, disclosure and operational
documentation prepared for the Hidden History application.

These documents are being prepared before public beta release.

They are not considered final until the complete pre-beta legal audit
has been completed.

---

## CURRENT HIDDEN HISTORY SCOPE

Hidden History currently includes:

- Free vehicle search
- Free vehicle/advert analysis
- Pro/paid vehicle search
- Official vehicle information
- MOT history information
- MOT test results
- MOT advisories
- MOT failures
- Saved vehicle search reports
- User profile/account functionality
- Affiliate links to vehicle-part suppliers

---

## FEATURES NOT CURRENTLY INCLUDED

The following are NOT currently part of Hidden History:

- Marketplace
- Vehicle marketplace
- Messaging system
- Community system
- Trader accounts
- Business accounts
- Recurring subscriptions
- Business subscription plans
- MIB/MIAFTR data access
- Other future vehicle-history databases

Where future functionality is mentioned in the legal documentation,
it must be clearly identified as future functionality.
