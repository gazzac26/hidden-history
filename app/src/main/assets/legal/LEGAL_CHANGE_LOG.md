ENTRY 005 — SEARCH RETENTION AND SAVED REPORT MODEL CONFIRMED

Date: 3 September 2026

Change:

The retention model was finalised.

Ordinary unsaved vehicle searches are not retained as permanent user
search history.

Where a user chooses to save a report, the relevant report information
is stored in Supabase and associated with the user's account.

Saved reports include a report-generation timestamp.

Users can delete saved reports.

Account deletion is intended to remove account-linked reports and
ordinary account data, subject to any limited retention that may be
legally necessary.

Generated PDF reports are created for the user's download/share action
and are not permanently stored by Hidden History.

Reason:

Reflect Hidden History's data-minimisation and limited-retention design
and ensure that the Privacy Policy, DPIA, retention documentation and
application behaviour remain aligned.

ENTRY 007 — DATA (USE AND ACCESS) ACT 2025 POSITION REVIEWED

Date: 3 September 2026

Change:

The Hidden History legal framework was reviewed against the current UK
data-protection position following the commencement of the Data (Use and
Access) Act 2025.

The ICO confirmed on 19 June 2026 that all data-protection provisions of
the Act were then in force.

The Act amends the existing UK data-protection framework rather than
replacing the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018.

Reason:

Ensure that the Hidden History documentation is based on the current UK
legal framework rather than an outdated pre-2026 GDPR template.

The ICO continues to update its guidance following the Act, so relevant
guidance must continue to be monitored.
