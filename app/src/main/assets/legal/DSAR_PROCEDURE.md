## 1. PURPOSE

This procedure explains how Hidden History handles requests for access to personal information.

A subject access request may be made without using the words “subject access request” or “SAR”.

---

## 2. REQUEST CONTACT

Requests may be sent to:

SovereignSoftwareLtd@gmail.com

---

## 3. REQUEST RECEIPT

On receipt:

1. Record the date received.
2. Identify the requester and request type.
3. Verify identity where reasonably necessary.
4. Clarify the request only where genuinely required.
5. Identify relevant systems and providers.
6. Search for relevant personal information.
7. Consider applicable exemptions or restrictions.
8. Prepare the response.
9. Record completion.

---

## 4. IDENTITY VERIFICATION

Verification must be proportionate to the risk of disclosure.

Do not request excessive identification information.

Where the requester is already authenticated through the Hidden History account, existing account information may assist with verification.

---

## 5. SYSTEMS TO CONSIDER

Depending on the request, search relevant Hidden History systems, which may include:

- User account/profile records
- Saved reports
- Account-linked advert-analysis records
- Relevant support records
- Relevant security records
- Payment/entitlement information actually held by Hidden History

Do not treat DVLA or DVSA as Hidden History's own database of personal information where the requested information is controlled by the external source.

---

## 6. THIRD-PARTY INFORMATION

Where personal information is held by a third party, determine whether:

- Hidden History is the controller for the relevant processing;
- The third party is a processor acting for Hidden History; or
- The third party is an independent controller.

The response must reflect the actual legal relationship.

---

## 7. EXEMPTIONS AND RESTRICTIONS

Before disclosure, consider whether any lawful exemption, restriction or limitation applies.

Do not withhold information merely because the request is inconvenient.

Where information cannot be disclosed, explain the position as far as legally permitted.

---

## 8. RESPONSE

The response must be provided within the applicable legal timeframe.

The response should normally include:

- The personal information held
- Relevant supplementary information required by law
- Any applicable explanation of processing
- Information about rights and complaints where required

---

## 9. FORMAT

Where practical, information should be supplied electronically in a commonly used format.

Security must be maintained when transmitting personal information.

---

## 10. REQUEST LOG

Maintain a secure record of:

- Date received
- Requester
- Verification
- Scope
- Systems searched
- Providers contacted where relevant
- Decision
- Date completed
- Any exemption/limitation relied upon

Keep only what is necessary to demonstrate and administer compliance.

---

## 11. ESCALATION

Escalate complex requests involving:

- Third-party controller disputes
- Legal claims
- Large or technically complex searches
- Suspected data breaches
- Repeated or abusive requests requiring legal assessment
- Regulator correspondence

---

## 12. REVIEW

Review this procedure when privacy law or the production data architecture changes.
