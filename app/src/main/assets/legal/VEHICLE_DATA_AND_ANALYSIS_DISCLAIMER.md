# HIDDEN HISTORY
# VEHICLE DATA AND ANALYSIS DISCLAIMER

Status: PRE-BETA / WORKING DRAFT

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Hidden History provides vehicle information and analysis intended to
help users research vehicles.

The service should be treated as an information and research tool.

It is not a substitute for an independent vehicle inspection or
professional advice.

---

## 2. DATA ACCURACY

Vehicle information is obtained from connected data sources.

Hidden History does not create the underlying official vehicle records.

Information may therefore be:

- Incomplete
- Delayed
- Incorrect
- Missing
- Presented differently from the original source

---

## 3. MOT INFORMATION

An MOT record represents information recorded at the time of the test.

It does not establish the vehicle's current condition.

A vehicle that has passed an MOT may subsequently develop faults.

A vehicle with advisories may have been repaired since the test.

---

## 4. MOT FAILURES AND ADVISORIES

Where available, Hidden History may display MOT failures and advisories.

These records should be considered historical information.

Users should inspect the vehicle and obtain appropriate professional
advice before purchase.

---

## 5. VEHICLE ANALYSIS

Hidden History may analyse vehicle information or advertisement content.

The resulting analysis is an aid to decision-making.

It is not a guarantee of:

- Vehicle quality
- Vehicle safety
- Mechanical condition
- Market value
- Legal status
- Ownership
- Accident history
- Insurance history
- Finance status
- Theft status

---

## 6. NO GUARANTEE OF FRAUD DETECTION

A vehicle may have fraud, misrepresentation or undisclosed problems
that are not visible in the available data.

The absence of a warning does not mean that a vehicle is free from
fraud or other risks.

---

## 7. INDEPENDENT CHECKS

Users should consider appropriate independent checks before purchasing
a vehicle.

These may include:

- Physical inspection
- Test drive
- Independent mechanic inspection
- Verification of seller identity
- Verification of vehicle documentation
- Appropriate history checks
- Verification of mileage
- Verification of service records

---

## 8. NO PROFESSIONAL ADVICE

Hidden History does not provide mechanical, legal, financial or
insurance advice merely by displaying or analysing vehicle information.
