## 1. PURPOSE

This document provides the framework for recording a legitimate interests assessment (LIA) where Hidden History proposes to rely on legitimate interests under the UK GDPR.

A legitimate interests assessment must be specific to the processing activity.

---

## 2. THREE-PART TEST

The assessment must consider:

1. Purpose test — Is there a legitimate interest?
2. Necessity test — Is the processing necessary for that interest?
3. Balancing test — Do the individual's interests, rights or freedoms override the legitimate interest?

The assessment must be documented before relying on the basis.

---

## 3. CURRENT POTENTIAL USES

Hidden History may have legitimate interests in limited processing such as:

- Maintaining service security
- Preventing abuse and fraud
- Protecting infrastructure
- Investigating security incidents
- Responding to support matters
- Defending or establishing legal claims
- Maintaining necessary operational reliability

Legitimate interests must not be used as a blanket basis for all processing.

## 5. PART 1 — PURPOSE TEST

Legitimate interest pursued:

Describe:

- Who benefits
- Why the interest exists
- Whether there is a wider public or user benefit
- Why the interest is genuine and present

Possible examples include security, fraud prevention or defending legal claims.

Do not describe commercial convenience alone as sufficient justification.

---

## 6. PART 2 — NECESSITY TEST

Assess:

- What processing is actually necessary?
- What information is necessary?
- Is each item of information proportionate?
- Could the same purpose reasonably be achieved by a less intrusive method?

If the processing is not necessary, legitimate interests cannot be relied upon for that activity.

---

## 7. PART 3 — BALANCING TEST

Consider:

- Reasonable expectations of users
- Nature and sensitivity of the information
- Volume and frequency
- Potential consequences
- Whether the processing is unexpected
- Whether it could cause harm
- Safeguards
- User rights
- Whether a less intrusive method exists

The assessment must explain why the legitimate interest is not overridden.

---

## 8. SAFEGUARDS

Possible safeguards include:

- Data minimisation
- Restricted access
- Limited retention
- Encryption
- Pseudonymisation where appropriate
- Security monitoring
- Transparency
- User rights
- Internal access controls

Record the safeguards actually implemented.

## 9. USER EXPECTATIONS

Hidden History's current operating model is deliberately privacy-minimised.

There is no general-purpose advertising or behavioural analytics system.

Personal information is not intended to be sold or used for unrelated profiling.

This may be relevant to reasonable expectations, but each LIA must still consider the particular processing.

## 12. REVIEW

Review the LIA if:

- Purpose changes
- Data categories change
- Processing becomes more intrusive
- New recipients are introduced
- User expectations materially change
- A new technology or provider is introduced
- A complaint or incident indicates the balancing assessment may no longer be appropriate
